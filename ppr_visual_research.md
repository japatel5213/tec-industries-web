# PPR Visual Research Notes

## Visual direction for TEC product imagery

External product references describe three-layer PPR systems as an inner PPR layer, a distinct reinforcing middle layer, and an outer PPR/PPR-C layer. The exact middle-layer material is not consistent across the references: some describe glass-fibre-reinforced PPR, while others describe aluminium. TEC’s approved product information confirms the product cue as **green and blue triple-layer PPR pipe**, but it does not approve a specific internal layer material or cross-section claim.

For that reason, generated photography should show authentic-looking green and blue pipe surfaces, clean cut ends, coordinated fittings, and industrial manufacturing/installation contexts. It should not show a labelled cross-section, imply a specific internal material, or add technical callouts about pressure, temperature, standards, service life, fibre, aluminium, certification, or performance. If a layered visual is used, it should remain an unlabelled abstract concentric construction cue and must not be presented as a technical specification.
